03 / 04

Less Energy is Better

In this assignment, we were asked to create a sustainable design, therefore I chose Dithering. The “Sailboat” visual depicts the “Low Energy” nature of Dithering. Sailboats use wind as their power source, which is an efficient and natural way to move. This symbolically reflects how dithering uses minimal computing resources—optimizing what is available in an efficient way to achieve a goal.
