Tian Banana Packaging

For my final project, I designed a packaging system for Tian Banana, a premium fried banana dessert brand inspired by Peranakan culture. The project includes individual packaging and collection boxes for various product variants. I combined traditional Peranakan motifs with custom illustrations and a contemporary visual approach to create a cohesive and premium packaging experience.

Good Waffle

In my college assignment, I was asked to redesign the logos of MSMEs around us. I chose Good Waffle as the brand that I will redesign. I also made the word "ff" with two shades of brown as a representation of waffles.
