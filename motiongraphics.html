Elia's Tea Party

This is a platformer game that i made for my final exam project. Elia is a bunny character that loves pink a lot! Elia often held tea parties at Clara’s Backyard and invited her friends. However, Elia forgot to stock her tea items and she had to go buy and collect them. Elia’s Tea Party promises a heartwarming adventure filled with charming soft color, delightful music, and a sprinkle of challenge to keep players of all ages eager for more!
