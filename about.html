Design is more than creating visually appealing pieces; it’s about solving problems and communicating effectively. My creative process begins with thorough research and brainstorming to ensure I deeply understand the project's goals. From there, I dive into sketching and exploring various concepts before refining my ideas into a cohesive design. I value collaboration and feedback, viewing them as essential to creating something truly impactful. Whether working on branding, illustration, or motion design, I bring precision, adaptability, and attention to detail to each project, ensuring every piece is thoughtful and polished.

My creative world is inspired by the timeless appeal of Japanese retro artists, where simplicity meets an expressive, nostalgic aesthetic. Minimalism also guides my approach, valuing clean lines, balanced compositions, and understated elegance in every design. I’m drawn to coquette elements—soft colors, delicate details, and a whimsical sense of charm—that add a unique touch of personality to my work. These influences shape my style, blending retro vibes, simplicity, and a hint of playful sophistication that makes each piece feel distinctively my own.

I’m committed to expanding my expertise in both graphic design and UI/UX, with a strong focus on understanding user needs and behaviors. My goal is to create designs that not only look captivating but are also intuitive and accessible, putting the user experience at the forefront. I believe that every project is an opportunity to learn, and I’m dedicated to continuously upgrading my skills and exploring new design techniques. As I move forward, I’m excited to combine my knowledge in graphic design with UI/UX insights, creating engaging, user-centered experiences that make a lasting impact.
